A56 Napoli is an independent information app for the A56 Tangenziale di Napoli. No account or demo credentials are required. The interface is in Italian. An internet connection is needed to refresh notices, display Apple Maps content, search places and calculate routes.

Testing:
1. Open Mappa, switch between the two directions and inspect the current notices and Programmate. Chiusure lists available scheduled notices. Notice availability depends on the provider; no active notice is not proof that every ramp is open.
2. Open Percorsi and enter a start and destination manually (for example, Pozzuoli and Capodichino). The app compares the driving routes returned by Apple Maps against available A56 notices. Alternative routes depend on Apple Maps and may not always be returned.
3. Location permission is optional. It is requested when selecting Usa la mia posizione or starting a trip from the current location to a favorite. Denying permission still allows a manually entered starting address. Location is not used in the background.
4. In Info, inspect the independent-service notice, data sources, privacy information and Archivio locale. Deleting the local notice history does not delete saved destinations or the current/future notice snapshot shared with the widget.
5. Add an A56 Napoli widget from the iPhone Home Screen widget gallery. Successful app refreshes publish the latest notices in an on-device App Group and request a widget reload. Widgets reuse this snapshot when fresh and also independently request notices when needed. They show data timestamps/status. iOS determines their refresh schedule; the app does not guarantee a refresh interval. The widget does not request or use location.

Cached, incomplete, expired or unavailable data is labeled. The app is intended for checking information before travel and is not an emergency service or turn-by-turn navigation system. Displayed times use Europe/Rome, including daylight-saving changes.

Traffic notices come from the provider endpoints identified in the release documentation. Apple Maps supplies maps, place search and driving routes. OpenStreetMap attribution is displayed for the derived A56 road geometry.
